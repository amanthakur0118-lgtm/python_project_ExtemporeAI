CONTEXT & CHALLENGES

The Problem: Impromptu Speaking & Interview Anxiety

Why traditional training fails candidates preparing for UPSC, SSB, and MBA interviews

⚠️ The Extempore Bottleneck
  • Crucial test in Civil Services, Defence (SSB), and MBA GD/PI admissions.
  • Candidates freeze when asked to deliver spontaneous, structured 3-to-5 minute speeches.
  • Panic pauses and unstructured rambling damage candidate confidence.
💸 Prohibitive Coaching Costs
  • Human mentors cost upwards of $100/hour, inaccessible to most students.
  • Peer feedback is subjective, unstructured, and lacks standard rubrics.
  • Zero metric tracking for Words-Per-Minute, pauses, and factual relevance.
📰 Static Knowledge Gap
  • Textbooks and past papers fail to reflect breaking global and legal events.
  • Candidates struggle to connect breaking news to constitutional frameworks.
  • No unified platform uniting news reading with speech delivery.

EVALUATION METRICS

50-Point Standardized UPSC Scoring Rubric

Multi-dimensional evaluation with strict anti-hallucination and incoherence safeguards

📊 5 Core Dimensions (10 Pts Each)
  • 1. Factual Accuracy & Context: Empirical data, constitutional articles, treaties.
  • 2. Relevance & Focus: Direct adherence to question; penalizes digressions.
  • 3. Structure & Clarity: Clear intro, balanced arguments, and solid conclusion.
  • 4. Depth of Analysis: Multi-stakeholder impacts and policy roadmaps.
  • 5. Time Discipline & Pacing: Sustained delivery within optimal 110-150 WPM.
🛡️ Quality Guards & Anti-Hallucination
  • Incoherence Guard: Random typing or gibberish awarded <5/50 with Disqualified status.
  • Off-Topic Penalty: Unrelated speeches heavily penalized in Relevance (1/10).
  • Ideal Model Answer: Generates gold-standard UPSC benchmark structure.
  • Orator Ranking: Categorizes candidate into Distinguished, Proficient, or Developing.

DATABASE & STORAGE

Relational Schema & Dual Failover Mirror

Engineered for data integrity, analytics reporting, and zero-configuration cloud portability

🗄️ Relational Schema Tables
  • users: Candidate credentials and profile metadata.
  • categories: Domain definitions, slugs, and icon descriptors.
  • headlines_cache: Cached RSS items with TTL to avoid redundant network calls.
  • practice_sessions: Speeches, duration, WPM, and full transcript logs.
  • evaluations: 5 rubric scores, orator tier, feedback, and model answer.
  • quiz_results: Domain, score, accuracy %, and time elapsed.
🔄 Dual MySQL & SQLite Mirror
  • MySQL 8.0 Primary: Full relational DDL with foreign keys and cascading deletes.
  • SQLite 3 Fallback: Seamless auto-initialization when MySQL credentials are omitted.
  • Serverless Sandboxing: Detects Vercel/Lambda and redirects database writes to /tmp/.
  • Completely eliminates read-only filesystem crashes in cloud serverless deployments.
